# Free Credit Radar — 免费额度雷达

**What is Free Credit Radar?** A tracker for AI free credits, free trials and new-user bonuses across AI services. Each entry has a provider, a category (Model API free credit, AI app free trial, Cloud free credit, Dev tools free, Free compute), an amount or detail, an official source link, an optional expiry date with a countdown, a Verified / Unverified status (Expired is computed automatically once the date passes) and a 0–10 score. You can search, filter by category, "New today" or "Ending soon", sort by expiry date or score, and copy the list as Markdown. Expired entries move to an "Expired / archived" section instead of being deleted. Entries and language are saved in the browser (localStorage).

**免费额度雷达是什么？** 追踪各家 AI 服务的免费额度、免费试用与新人福利。每个条目有提供方、分类（模型 API 免费额度、AI 应用免费试用、云厂商免费额度、开发工具免费、免费算力）、金额或详情、官网来源链接、可选到期日与倒计时、已核验 / 未核验状态（过期由系统按日期自动判定）与 0–10 评分。支持搜索、按分类 / 今日新增 / 即将到期筛选、按到期日或评分排序、一键复制 Markdown 清单。过期条目会移入「已过期 / 归档」区而不是被删除。条目与语言保存在浏览器本地存储中。

## Honest limits / 诚实说明

- This is a static page: it cannot check offers for you and it never auto-refreshes. Every entry is maintained by hand.
- The four entries shipped with the template are clearly marked samples with no source links. Verify any offer on its official page before claiming, and replace the samples with your own findings.
- Free offers change fast and may end early, change terms, or require a card or region. The expiry countdown is only as good as the date you typed in.

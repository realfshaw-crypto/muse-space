# Family Schedule Hub — 家庭日程中心

**What is Family Schedule Hub?** A weekly hub for the whole family: add an event with the person it is for, the day, the time and who handles it — school pickups, practices, errands — and see the week at a glance, filtered by person.

**家庭日程中心是什么？** 全家一周日程中心：添加事项时写清是给谁的、星期几、几点、由谁负责——接送、训练、跑腿——一周安排一眼看清，还能按人筛选。

# Portfolio Tracker — 投资组合

**What is Portfolio Tracker?** A manual portfolio tracker: add stocks, funds, crypto and cash by hand, see total value, total gain and loss, and how your allocation compares with your targets. All prices are entered by you — this template does not fetch market data.

**投资组合是什么？** 一款手动记账式投资组合工具：手动添加股票、基金、加密货币与现金，查看组合总价值、总盈亏，以及实际配置与目标配置的对比。所有价格均由你手动填写，本模板不拉取任何行情数据。

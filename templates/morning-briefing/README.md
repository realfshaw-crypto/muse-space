# Morning Briefing — 晨间简报

**What is Morning Briefing?** One page for the start of the day: today's agenda with times, top tasks to check off, an email triage board (act now, reply later, archive) and a short news digest with one line on why each story matters to you. Sample data included — replace it with your own day.

**晨间简报是什么？** 一天开始只看这一页：今日日程带时间、可勾选的要事、邮件分拣板（马上处理、稍后回复、归档），以及每条附一句「跟你有什么关系」的新闻摘要。带示例数据，换成你自己的一天。

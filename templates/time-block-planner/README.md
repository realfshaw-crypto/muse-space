# Time Block Planner — 周时间块

**What is Time Block Planner?** A weekly time-blocking planner: lay out your week as blocks of time on a Mon–Sun grid, 07:00–23:00, in five categories — work, study, health, life and rest — with per-category hours and share, a highlighted today column, and one-click duplication of a block to another day.

**周时间块是什么？** 一款按周排时间块的规划器：周一到周日、每天 07:00–23:00 的网格上排布时间块，分工作、学习、健康、生活、休息五类，带每类小时数与占比、当日列高亮，一键把时间块复制到另一天。

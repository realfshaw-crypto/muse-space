# Deal Hunter — 折扣猎手

**What is Deal Hunter?** A price watchlist for things you want: add an item with its store, list price, current price and the price you are willing to pay, and it shows the discount, how much you save and a "Buy now" badge once the price you logged hits your target. Prices are entered by hand — it never fetches live prices. Log each price change with a date to keep a small price history, sort by discount, savings, expiry or name, and filter to ready-to-buy items only. Each deal can carry a category (shopping, AI credits, cloud & VPS, subscriptions, other), an expiry date with an ending-soon badge, a source link and a verified flag — check the official page before you buy; expired deals stay visible but faded.

**折扣猎手是什么？** 一份心仪商品的价格关注清单：添加商品时写下商店、原价、当前价和你愿意付的目标价，它会算出折扣、已省金额，记录的价格一旦降到目标价就亮起「可以下手」标记。价格全部手动填写，不拉取实时价格。每次价格变动按日期记一笔，形成小段价格记录；可按折扣、省钱、截止或名称排序，也可只看可以下手的商品。每条优惠可以带分类（购物、AI 额度、云服务、订阅、其他）、截止日期与即将截止标记、来源链接与核实标记——下单前先去官方页面核对，过期优惠会保留但变淡显示。
